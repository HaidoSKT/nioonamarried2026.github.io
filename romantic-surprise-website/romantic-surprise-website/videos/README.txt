Drop your romantic video here!
For example:
- video.mp4 (iPhone / Android video, TikTok clip, romantic compilation)

Supported formats:
- .mp4 (recommended)
- .webm
- .mov

Then in config.js:
  video: {
    enabled: true,
    title: "A Special Video For You 🎥",
    url: "videos/video.mp4",
    caption: "Our favorite moments together 🎬💖"
  }

Or you can use a YouTube video link:
  url: "https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YOUR_VIDEO_ID"
